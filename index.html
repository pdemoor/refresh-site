Arrival is broken

You arrive. But you are not ready.

Your body has arrived, but your mind is still catching up. And yet you are expected to step straight into your day: a meeting, a city, a moment that matters.

Travel has been optimised for speed. But almost nobody has designed for arrival.

The story of our logo

Calm, purposeful, and designed for long journeys.

The sea turtle became part of Refresh only after the idea was already formed. For years, we have spent time around the Great Barrier Reef and across Polynesia, where sea turtles are a constant presence. There is something distinctive about the way they move — calm, unhurried, and always purposeful. Sea turtles travel vast distances across open ocean, yet somehow always arrive exactly where they need to be. That feeling aligned perfectly with what Refresh represents.

Refresh was created for a future where travel becomes quieter, smoother, and more effortless — where the journey begins to solve itself, but arrival still matters. The turtle carries its own protected space wherever it goes. It is steady, enduring, and designed for long journeys. Those qualities became part of our philosophy, our DNA, and ultimately our logo. Because Refresh is not simply about travel. It is about arriving well.
